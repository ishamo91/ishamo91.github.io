Dead Man's Dock

A first-person wave survival shooter. Hold a pirate dock on a tropical island against ten waves of skeletons, and spend what you earn between rounds.

Engine
Unreal Engine 5
Genre
Wave survival FPS
My part
Gameplay, UI, level setup
Video
1 min 18 s

The game

You start from a main menu with graphics options, then defend the dock with a pistol. Each wave sends more skeletons than the last. Between waves a countdown runs, and you can open the shop with the coins from your kills.

The island is lit for bright daylight, with docks, a pirate ship, palm trees, cannons and a plane wreck to fight around.

  • Wave directorTen waves, growing enemy counts, a skeletons-left tracker and a timer between waves.
  • Skeleton AIEnemies path across the beach and dock toward the player, with health bars over their heads.
  • Coin economyKills pay coins, and the B key opens a shop between waves.
  • CombatPistol with a 15-round magazine, reloading and a player health bar.
  • MenusPlay, Options and Quit, built with UMG.
  • Graphics optionsLow to Ultra for anti-aliasing, post-processing, textures, effects, foliage and shading.

Cup Zone Wars

A competitive zone wars island for Fortnite, built around a lobby hub where points, gold and trophies carry across rounds.

Engine
UEFN
Mode
Solo zone wars
My part
Lobby, systems, round flow
Video
23 s

The island

Players spawn into a themed hub with rules boards, map billboards and a giant trophy. A live leaderboard tracks the session, and a loadout wall lets players pick their weapons or roll a random set.

Rounds play out on a desert map with a moving storm. After round 10, the player with the most points takes the trophy.

  • Live leaderboardTracks every player's cup points across the session.
  • Cup pointsPoints for Victory Royales, placement brackets and each elimination.
  • Gold economy+5 per Victory Royale, +1 per elimination and +1 per minute played.
  • Trophy systemMost points after round 10 earns a trophy.
  • Loadout boardPick weapons from a wall, or hit the dice to randomize.
  • Stats HUDTrophies, gold and points stay on screen.

Customizable 1v1

A 1v1 build fight map where players set up the whole match from inside the game, without opening a single menu.

Engine
UEFN
Mode
1v1 build fights
My part
Solo, everything
Video
48 s

The map

Every setting lives in the world. Players walk up to panels to set health and shields, change the sky, pick a loadout or open a full crosshair editor, then go straight into the fight.

The arena has ramps, a stair course and open space for building practice.

  • Crosshair editor12 styles, a size slider, a full color spectrum, presets, Random and Reset.
  • Health presetsOne-tap 200, 180 or 165, with fine-tune sliders for shield and health.
  • Sky switcherChange the island's sky and mood on the fly.
  • Loadout wallWeapons picked straight off a display.
  • Score trackerA live scoreboard for each 1v1.
  • Settings slidersIn-world percentage sliders for match options.
